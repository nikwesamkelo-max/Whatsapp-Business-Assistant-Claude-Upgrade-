Business Hours

We are open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Booking Policy

A 30 percent deposit is required to confirm any booking. The remaining balance is due 48 hours before the event date.

Bookings made less than 5 days before the event are subject to a rush fee and availability is not guaranteed.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made more than 14 days before the event receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations made within 14 days forfeit the deposit but no further charges apply.

Delivery Area

We currently deliver and set up within a 40km radius of our warehouse. Deliveries outside this radius may incur an additional transport fee, calculated per kilometer.

Damage Policy

Customers are responsible for equipment during the rental period. Minor wear is expected, but lost or significantly damaged items will be charged at replacement cost.
