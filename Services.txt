Our Services

We provide full event rental and setup services for weddings, corporate functions, birthdays, and private parties.

Table and chair packages start at R500 and include 60-inch round tables and white folding chairs, delivered and set up on site.

Tent rentals include a 20x20 frame tent suitable for up to 80 guests standing or 50 seated. Larger tents are available on request with extra lead time.

Lighting packages include warm white string lights and can be added to any table or tent package for an additional fee.

Linen rental includes white round tablecloths in 120 inch size, ironed and ready for setup.

Catering equipment such as chafing dishes and serving stations can be rented separately or bundled with a full event package.

Audio equipment includes portable PA speakers suitable for toasts, announcements, and background music at small to medium events.

We do not provide catering food or bar service directly, but we can recommend trusted local partners on request.
